# Converge a design

Use this for a design or a plan before code exists. Use it when the design is big enough to deserve it. Skip it for small work. Code PRs go through Shipping instead.

1. Lay out the whole issue. Say what we want to do. Build the design first.
2. Get the reviewers up to speed on the issue. Take them from the `design reviewers` line in the role file. One reviewer runs per entry, each from a different model family. Have each reviewer go through the design and give feedback.
3. Take in all the feedback. Verify anything they found that you were not aware of. Check it against the code or the data before you act on it.
4. Incorporate anything you agree with and think is correct. Fix it or change it in the design. Push back on anything you disagree with.
5. Hold your ground with a reviewer who is not up to speed. If a reviewer gives you good stuff, look at it clearly. Then make your ruling.
6. Go back and forth with the same reviewers until you converge.
7. Bring the converged design to the operator. Go through it and discuss it together.

You make the rulings. The reviewers inform them.
